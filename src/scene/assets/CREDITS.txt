These are historical visual reference composites, not weather forecasts or
current imagery for the selected mission date. The launch landscape is a
presentation backdrop, not a surveyed reconstruction. Procedural local cloud
and exhaust textures are original artwork informed by NASA launch imagery.
Original project files in src/data/ remain unchanged.
