These are historical visual reference composites, not weather forecasts or
current imagery for the selected mission date. The launch landscape is a
presentation backdrop, not a surveyed reconstruction. Static launch-pad cloud
art is original artwork informed by NASA launch imagery.
Original project files in src/data/ remain unchanged.

Real launch cinematic: artemis-launch.mp4.
Historical Artemis II launch footage, April 1, 2026. Credit: NASA.
Edited into a silent 9.5-second, 1280 x 720, 30 fps H.264 montage.
The original camera framing and source playback speed are preserved; footage
is not a simulation of the selected mission date, launch vehicle performance,
or selected mission requirements. Hard cuts omit time between flight phases.
